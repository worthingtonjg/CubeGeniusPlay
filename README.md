# Cube Genius Play

Playable development preview of Cube Genius, built with Unity.

Play at https://worthingtonjg.github.io/CubeGeniusPlay/.

On a phone, rotate to landscape to play. Portrait shows a rotate screen and pauses gameplay. Returning to landscape releases only that orientation pause; an existing manual or focus pause still needs Resume. The game keeps its landscape layout and aspect ratio.

Enter Tutorial Mode to begin: a solved cube shuffles automatically before the lesson starts. A mixed or partially solved cube keeps its current position. Reloading during the initial shuffle restores the solved cube in free play, ready to try again. Orientation steps before the first and after the last puzzle-layer turn in each series run automatically. Orientation between puzzle turns stays visible and manual: use the current move card's Play control or drag the cube. You can also bypass intervening view steps by manually making the physically equivalent next face turn from your current view; guidance then adapts to that orientation. All rotations remain undoable and are excluded from the puzzle move total.

The preview includes case-specific cross explanations, visible middle-edge alignment and mirrored insertion, always-open scrollable explanations, stage and layer guidance, selection highlighting with expanding square sticker ripples and a pulsating border, and completion feedback. A wrong move can be undone or kept while exiting Tutorial. Returning from a hidden tab requires Resume.

This repository contains only compiled runtime files, game assets and notices. Puzzle progress is saved locally in your browser. Updates are published on demand.
